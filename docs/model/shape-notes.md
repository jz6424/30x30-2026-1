# Aphrona Halo – shape notes (from the 9 listing photos)

Only 4 of the 9 photos show the device: 61O3u (3/4 view on a face), 71nP77 (front view),
71RvkB (4-in-1 detail grid), 818YbM (inside view, LEDs on). halo-1 is a front render.
71S4tz, 81FX1o, 61dKcK and halo-red are infographics or before/after photos and show no geometry.

## Overall form
- A **rigid hard shell** (glossy white plastic), not a soft silicone sheet. It is a deep "helmet visor":
  it wraps the face from the forehead back to the ears and down past the chin.
- **Outline (front):** a tall rounded "U" / shield. The top edge is a broad, slightly flattened arc
  at the hairline. The sides run nearly straight down past the ears, then taper into a rounded chin.
  Width:height is about 0.75 for the face part.
- **Front face:** a large **mirror-gold / bronze tinted visor** inset inside a white rim (roughly 4–6 % of the width).
  The visor is see-through ("see through to fit your lifestyle"). The front render shows two
  lighter oval **eye windows** at about 40 % down. A darker vertical **nose/mouth breathing window**
  runs down the centre line ("window of the nose and mouth"). No open holes show from the front.
- **Side profile (61O3u):** the white shell has a swooping S-curve. The gold visor sweeps down and
  forward to the chin, and the white body behind it sweeps back toward the ear.
- **Thickness:** the shell wall is a few mm. The body is deep: the white side walls are about 25–30 % of
  the face width front to back.

## Temple pods / button
- A white rounded-rectangle **pod at each temple** (just above ear level), sticking out about 1/3
  of the shell depth. It carries the "A" logo / **touch power button** (hand icon in 71RvkB).
  The front render shows a pod on both sides; 71RvkB-03 shows a round button boss on the side.

## Neck piece
- **NeckCover:** a separate, magnetically attached white collar ("Dual cover: face-only or face+neck").
  It is a half-cylinder / U-shaped skirt that flares slightly downward. Its top edge follows the chin
  curve and is about as wide as the lower face, about 0.45–0.5 of the face height tall.
  Gold-tone contact strips run along its top rim. Inside it is black with LEDs.

## Strap
- **Double bands** (71RvkB-02, 818YbM): two light-grey/white rubber straps with punched holes
  and a ring buckle. They attach at the temple pods on the inside and wrap around the back of the head.

## Inside / LEDs
- The inner surface is **matte black**. LEDs are small round red emitters on a regular
  **grid (rows and columns about 1 LED diameter apart)**, covering forehead, cheeks, jaw and also the
  inside of the neck cover. The listing says **807 LED chips**.
- There are no LEDs in the eye windows or in the centre nose/mouth strip (black gap in 818YbM).
- **LEDs appear only on the inside.** The outside is white plastic plus the gold visor.

## Colour
- Shell: glossy white / warm off-white (#F2EFEA). Visor: polished gold-bronze metallic (#C79A5E → #8A5A2E).
- Inner liner: matte black. LEDs: red (630 nm) emissive; also blue/orange modes.

## Modelling decisions vs. the brief
- The brief says "white silicone shell with eye and mouth openings". The photos show a rigid white shell
  with a gold see-through visor and a nose/mouth breathing window, so I model what the photos show:
  white `Shell`, gold `Visor`, eye windows and a nose/mouth slot as separate glass/opening geometry,
  with no LEDs in those zones.

## v2: matched to the HALO PRO campaign photo
The owner's reference (three models wearing the HALO PRO) shows a different front from the listing renders:
- **Shell:** a rounded, satin off-white helmet front shaped like a superellipsoid egg. It wraps to just in front of the ears and has large fillets everywhere.
- **Visor:** ONE continuous egg-shaped mirror-gold visor recessed into a thick, rounded white rim. The rim is thin at the top and thicker at the sides and chin. There are no eye holes and no nose slot.
- **MouthSlit:** a thin dark crescent across the lower third that curves up at the ends.
- **Pods:** horizontal capsules at the temples with an oval touch plate (embossed "A" on one) and a strap rod out the back.
- **Strap:** slim grey bands, one over the crown and one around the back of the head.
- **NeckCover:** a separate bib that continues down from the chin and flares forward toward the collarbone. Its bottom edge is lower at the front.
